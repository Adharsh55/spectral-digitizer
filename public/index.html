💡 Modular Search Philosophy

This platform uses a keyless Bing Images search scraper for automatic chemical spectrum retrieval. Under the guidance of our IIT Madras mentor, the Search Module is completely decoupled from the digitization engine, making it trivial to plug in other sources (like Brave Search, SerpAPI, or offline PDF indexers) by editing only the search routes in server.js.
